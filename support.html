在安卓设备上只读查看 CDXML 原稿，缩放、分页浏览并导出 PDF。

请发邮件至 jessicawang04@163.com，注明设备型号、Android 及应用版本、复现步骤和可见错误提示。优先使用去除敏感内容的示例，不要发送保密实验原稿、Google 密码或验证码。支持与商店评分互相独立，不要求好评。

打开和阅读文件

CDXML Reader 适用于 Android 7.0 及以上，支持 CDXML 文件，单文件最多 10 MiB。使用应用内“打开”按钮或在其他应用中通过系统分享打开。可查看整图、原稿分页、缩放平移、重开最近文件及分享原始附件。

云盘文件须先由系统文件提供方下载。成功导入后，本机副本可离线阅读；云端文件更新后请重新导入。最近列表不跨设备同步，删除本机副本不改动来源原稿。

格式范围与 PDF

应用只读查看 CDXML，不编辑原稿，也不支持 CDX、MOL、SDF、RXN 或三维显示。复杂 ChemDraw 特殊对象、富文本、图片或布局可能显示不完整，请核对重要稿件。

PDF 支持原稿分页、整图和自定义分页，输出最高 300 DPI 的图像页，最多 100 页；不是可搜索文字或可编辑矢量。自定义分页方案退出后不保存。原文件分享保留导入时的字节。保存或分享前请核对内容、页数及接收对象。

应用本体在 Google Play 一次性付费下载，无广告、订阅或应用内购买。价格及实际付款以 Google Play 显示为准。测试者获得的应用本体促销码由 Google Play 兑换；每个码只能使用一次，并受有效期、账号资格和适用条款约束。测试邀请与兑换码各有用途，请按照收到的说明操作。

重新安装请使用获取应用时的 Google 账号；Play 获取记录不自动恢复本机文档副本或最近列表。订单和退款请通过 Google Play 处理。开发者不需要您的银行卡、Google 密码或验证码。

Support

Read CDXML documents on Android, navigate pages, zoom and export PDFs.

Contact the developer

Email jessicawang04@163.com with your device model, Android and app versions, steps to reproduce the problem and the visible error. Prefer sanitized examples; do not send confidential research, Google passwords or verification codes. Support is independent of store ratings and does not require a positive review.

Open and read files

CDXML Reader runs on Android 7.0 or later and supports CDXML files up to 10 MiB. Use Open in the app or system sharing from another app. View the whole drawing or source pages, zoom and pan, reopen recent files and share the original attachment.

Your system file provider must download cloud files first. Imported local copies work offline; import again after a cloud edit. Recents do not synchronize across devices, and removing a local copy does not change the source.

Formats and PDF export

This is a read-only CDXML viewer, without document editing, CDX, MOL, SDF, RXN or 3D viewing. Complex ChemDraw objects, rich text, images or layouts may be incomplete; verify important documents.

PDF export supports source pages, the whole drawing and custom pagination, with image pages up to 300 DPI and 100 pages per export. It does not produce searchable text or editable vectors. Custom pagination is not retained after leaving. Sharing the original file preserves imported bytes. Check content, page count and recipients before saving or sharing.

Purchases, test promo codes and reinstalls

The app is a one-time paid download on Google Play, without ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Google Play displays the applicable price and payment details. Testers' paid-app promo codes are redeemed through Google Play; each code can be used once, subject to dates, account eligibility and terms. Test invitations and promo codes serve different purposes; follow the instructions provided.

Reinstall with the Google account that acquired the app. Play acquisition records do not automatically restore local documents or recents. Use Google Play for orders and refunds. The developer does not need your bank card, Google password or verification codes.

Feedback, reviews and privacy

Useful feedback explains what you expected and what happened, with sanitized screenshots where needed. Review invitations are skippable, without rewards or required star ratings. The app itself does not request internet permission; Google Play, cloud providers, email and browsers may still connect using their own permissions. Reading and PDF generation occur on-device without uploading documents to the developer.
