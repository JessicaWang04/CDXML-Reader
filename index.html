1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the CDXML Reader Android app and its privacy and support pages. It provides read-only CDXML viewing and PDF export, without document editing, an app account, subscriptions, advertising or in-app purchases. The developer and privacy contact uses the name jessicawang04; contact jessicawang04@163.com.

This policy does not replace the independent policies of Google Play, cloud providers, email or messaging services, or sharing recipients. Their processing is described separately below.

2. Information and purposes

Reading in the app requires no account, name, phone number, identity document or payment credentials. The current app has no developer-operated document server, advertising SDK, or developer-integrated remote behavior analytics or crash reporting. Local documents may still contain personal or confidential information; check your authority to use, keep and share them.

3. File selection and system permissions

The app uses Android's document picker and files actively shared to it by other apps. It reads the selected or received file to process it, rather than scanning the whole device. Providers may be local storage, removable storage or cloud services.

The release app does not request INTERNET, contacts, location, camera, microphone, advertising-ID or all-files-management permissions, and does not integrate in-app billing. Independent file providers, Google Play, browsers and email apps may still connect using their own permissions. The app's lack of internet permission does not mean those services are offline. Cancelling file selection does not send the file to the developer.

4. Local processing and cloud snapshots

Parsing, display and PDF generation occur on-device. Imported original-byte copies support reopening and sharing. Reading and PDF export do not overwrite the source; the app does not pass documents, document content or hashes to Google SDKs or developer servers.

Cloud providers must first download unavailable cloud files under their own account, network and privacy terms. Imported copies are snapshots, without automatic updates after cloud edits or developer-server synchronization of recents. The app provides no remote conversion, cloud parsing or cloud document backup.

5. PDF export, sharing and recipients' copies

Only your save or share action hands a generated PDF or original attachment to a destination, app or service you choose. Files may contain source text, images, structures, names and related information. The app does not identify or remove all sensitive content for you; check the content, recipient and confidentiality requirements before proceeding.

Recipients may save, upload or forward files under their policies. Cancelling a share screen does not guarantee recall of copies already handed over. Attachments may remain in app cache; removing a library copy does not erase source files, separately saved PDFs or recipients' data. See section 9.

6. Google Play purchases, redemptions, reviews and platform services

CDXML Reader is a one-time paid download on Google Play, without subscriptions or additional in-app unlocks. Paid-app promo codes offered to testers are redeemed by Google Play subject to their dates, account eligibility and terms; they are not in-app purchases or subscription trials. Google Play handles app pricing, eligibility, orders, redemptions and refunds. The app receives no card numbers or payment passwords and does not verify purchases in-app or cache purchase tokens, transaction signatures or subscription status.

The developer may receive order information, redemption statistics, reviews or aggregate operational information made available through Play Console. Google manages Google accounts and Play acquisition records; these are not app accounts or a document-sync service. Reading, PDF export and support do not depend on leaving a review.

Review invitations are optional and skippable, without rewards or required star ratings. Google Play In-App Review processes ratings and text you enter. Published reviews may appear publicly; closed-test feedback may be shared privately with the developer. The app cannot learn from a completion callback whether you rated it or how many stars you gave. Use Google Play to edit or delete reviews.

Google Play and Android may also process download, diagnostic or security information under their policies, system settings and your choices. This is separate from uploading documents to the developer. See Google's Privacy Policy, In-App Review data disclosures and Google Play promotion information.

7. Voluntary support and test registration

Contacting jessicawang04@163.com provides your email address, message and any files, screenshots, device or version information you choose to include, for replies, troubleshooting and privacy requests. Email providers also process transmission and storage.

If you volunteer for testing, the developer may receive your Google Play sign-in email and feedback by email or private message to maintain Play tester lists, send instructions or promo codes, and arrange feedback. Do not provide Google passwords, bank cards, verification codes or identity documents. Contact the developer to leave the tester list; independent records held by email, messaging and Google Play services must be addressed through those services.

Provide only what is needed, preferably a sanitized example, rather than confidential research you cannot disclose. Support and testing contact information is retained only as needed for the request, test administration, follow-up and applicable obligations. When no longer needed, it is deleted or de-identified; you may also request deletion.

8. Website, cookies and GitHub Pages

These privacy and support pages are static, require no login, and contain no advertising, visitor-analytics scripts or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub Pages hosts the site; visiting requires your browser to connect to GitHub. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security whether or not they are signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Website access records and local app documents are separate processing activities. Merely visiting this page does not attach your documents, document content or hashes to requests to GitHub. Changes to hosting or analytics would require corresponding policy updates.

9. Retention, cache and deletion

  • Documents, file information and recents: kept until removed in the app, app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, without fixed-day automatic document eviction. Removing a local copy does not delete the source or separately imported, saved or shared copies.
  • Temporary imports, PDFs and share cache: cleaned through app workflows but may remain after interruption; the system may also clear cache. Shared attachments may remain until a later share or cache cleanup. Android settings can clear cache; clearing app data also removes the library and local settings.
  • Review-invitation records: kept locally to reduce repeat prompts; clearing app data or uninstalling removes these app records.
  • Support and testing contacts: kept for the necessary periods described in section 7. Legal requirements or unresolved requests may require limited retention.
  • Third parties: uninstalling does not delete Play orders, redemptions or reviews, cloud originals, email or messaging records, GitHub logs or recipients' copies. Use the relevant platform's controls or request channels.

The app disables Android automatic backup. Device vendors and backup or migration tools you use may behave differently; check their settings. Local deletion is not a promise of forensic secure erasure.

10. Sharing, sale and third-party components

We do not sell documents, reading records, support or testing contact information or use it for advertising profiles or cross-app tracking. Local parsing and drawing components do not send documents to their open-source maintainers. Your selected sharing, cloud, Google Play, website, email and messaging services are described separately above.

Information we actually hold may be provided to competent authorities where legally required, limited to what is necessary as permitted by applicable law. The app has no developer backend for remotely retrieving your device's documents.

11. Security

Measures include Android app-private storage, selected-file authorizations, file-size and model-complexity limits, and content-integrity checks. Hashes check integrity and are not document encryption; the app provides no document-password or end-to-end encryption service.

Device security, system permissions, backups, migration and recipients also affect confidentiality. No measure guarantees absolute security; protect your device and handle confidential information carefully.

12. Choices and privacy requests

You can refrain from importing, sharing, reviewing or registering for testing, cancel unfinished operations, and remove local records as described in section 9. You do not need to send an on-device document to the developer to delete it locally.

For information you voluntarily provided and we actually hold, contact jessicawang04@163.com to request information, access, correction or deletion. Other rights, such as restriction, withdrawal of withdrawable consent, portability or complaints, apply as provided by applicable law. We may request minimal information to verify your identity. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing or delete independently held third-party records.

CDXML Reader is a general chemical-file tool without child accounts, child profiling or advertising. Reading requires no birth date or proof of age. Minors providing support information, volunteering for testing or using third-party services should follow local requirements and obtain guardian assistance where needed. Guardians may contact us about information a minor voluntarily supplied.

Local reading does not require documents to be sent to a developer's overseas server. Your chosen cloud, email, messaging, Google Play, GitHub or sharing services may process information outside your country under their policies and safeguards. We do not promise that all third-party data remains in one country. Additional disclosures, legal bases or consent will be addressed where required.

When features, services or applicable requirements change, we will update this policy and its effective date, and provide prominent notice or obtain consent where required. Installation or continued use is not a blanket substitute for legally required separate notice or consent. Contact jessicawang04@163.com for privacy questions and requests.
