文件在本机处理

CDXML Reader 读取您主动选择或分享给应用的 CDXML 文件，在设备上保存文档副本、文件名称和最近阅读记录，供离线使用。 应用不向开发者上传文档，不提供账号、广告或行为追踪服务。

云盘文件

当您选择云盘文件时，由相应的系统文件提供方完成下载，其隐私政策适用。导入后的本地副本不会自动与云端同步。

分享和导出

只有您主动分享时，原始文件或生成的 PDF 才交给所选应用。接收方按其政策处理附件。 PDF 为图像页。分享插件可能在应用缓存内保留附件，移除最近文件不保证清除分享缓存或接收方副本。可通过 Android 设置清除应用缓存/数据。

购买和评价

付费下载由 Google Play 处理，Reader 不接收银行卡或付款密码。评价自愿，无奖励或强制五星。 阅读次数与提醒时间只保存在本机，用于减少提醒。Google Play 及其 SDK 根据其政策处理商店、评价和安全相关数据。

保存与删除

本地文档保留至应用内移除、清除应用数据或卸载。移除副本不删除原始来源文件。 临时导入/导出会清理，异常中断时可能暂留。应用关闭 Android 自动备份，系统/厂商迁移行为可能不同。应用没有账号，因而没有账号注销步骤。

Local documents

CDXML Reader processes selected CDXML files on your device and stores original-byte copies and recents. There is no account, advertising, behavioral tracking or developer-operated document server. Documents are not uploaded to the developer. File names and recent-reading records remain on your device for offline use.

Cloud files

Your selected system file provider downloads cloud files under its own privacy policy. Imported copies do not automatically synchronize with the cloud.

Sharing and export

Only your explicit sharing action hands an original attachment or generated image-based PDF to the selected recipient. The recipient's policy applies. Attachment copies may remain in the app's share cache. Removing a recent document does not guarantee removal of share-cache or recipient copies. Use Android settings to clear app cache/data.

Purchases and reviews

Google Play handles paid downloads; Reader does not receive your card or payment credentials. Reviews are optional, with no incentives or five-star requirement. Reading counts and invitation timestamps stay local to reduce reminders. Google Play and its SDK process store, review and security data under their policies.

Retention and deletion

Imported copies remain until removed in the app, app data is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Removing a copy does not delete the source. Temporary imports/exports are cleaned but may remain after interruption. Android automatic backup is disabled; system/vendor migration behavior may vary. There is no app account to delete.
