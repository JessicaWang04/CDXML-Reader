打开文件

支持 CDXML / CDX / MOL / SDF / RXN，单文件最多 10 MiB。使用应用内“打开”或系统分享。 云端文件必须先由文件提供方下载；导入后离线阅读。云端修改后重新导入，最近列表不跨设备同步。

离线三维

MOL/SDF 的三维预览由本地打包的 3Dmol.js 2.5.5 提供，仅读取文件已有坐标，不下载脚本或上传模型。二维坐标不自动生成构象；不提供 CIF/晶胞显示。支持旋转、缩放、球棍、空间填充、线框和 SDF 记录切换，每条三维记录最多 2,000 原子。

格式范围与 PDF

只读查看，不编辑原稿。复杂 ChemDraw 对象可能显示不完整，请核对重要稿件。 多格式版支持普通 V2000/V3000 MOL、最多 200 条 SDF 与 V2000 RXN；不保证特殊查询、V3000 续行和 agents。 原文件分享保留导入时的字节。PDF 支持原稿分页、整图或自定义分页，为最高 300 DPI 的图像页；不包含可搜索文字或可编辑矢量。分页设置退出后不保存。

Support

Email jessicawang04@163.com with device model, Android/app version, steps and visible error. Avoid confidential documents. Support is independent of ratings.

Open files

Use the app picker or system sharing for CDXML / CDX / MOL / SDF / RXN, up to 10 MiB per file. Your file provider must download cloud files first. Imported copies work offline; import again after a cloud edit. Recents stay on this device.

Offline 3D

Bundled 3Dmol.js 2.5.5 displays existing MOL/SDF coordinates locally. No scripts are downloaded and no models are uploaded. Flat coordinates are not converted into generated conformers. There is no CIF or crystal-cell support. Rotate, zoom, change styles and switch SDF records, up to 2,000 atoms per 3D record.

Formats and PDF

This is a read-only viewer. Complex ChemDraw objects may be incomplete; verify important documents. The multi-format product handles ordinary V2000/V3000 MOL, up to 200 SDF records and V2000 RXN, without guarantees for advanced queries, V3000 continuation rows or agents. Original sharing preserves imported bytes. PDF supports source, entire-canvas and custom pagination, using image pages up to 300 DPI. It is not searchable text or editable vectors. Pagination drafts are not retained after leaving.
